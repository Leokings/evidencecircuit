Title
EvidenceCircuit v2 — Three-valued evidence predicate VM

Notes / Description
EvidenceCircuit v2 is an MIT-licensed reusable GenLayer predicate VM. Builders freeze up to 12 evidence labels and 24 acyclic NOT/AND/OR/XOR/ATLEAST2 gates. Validators independently classify every label against a bounded public text packet, require verbatim quotes, and agree on the complete normalized assessment vector. Deterministic code executes three-valued logic, binds circuit/evidence digests, and stores caller-scoped immutable runs. Missing evidence stays UNKNOWN, even through NOT, and cannot satisfy either Boolean authorization check.

A fresh StudioNet deployment finalized six real-model cases covering support, contradiction, missing evidence, negation and prompt injection. Replay and invalid references finalized as expected failures. Includes direct tests, five-validator local scenarios, exact deployed-source proof and a wallet-free read-only verifier. It interprets supplied public text, not independently authenticated facts, and moves no funds.
